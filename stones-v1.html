How dissolution works
Uric acid stones dissolve when urine pH stays above 6.5. Hours per day at high pH matter more than morning pH alone.

Dissolution rate by pH:
≤5.5: 0% 6.0: 20% 6.5: 60% 7.0: 100% 7.2: 115%

Calibration: at pH 6.5 sustained 24h, dissolution rate ≈ 0.6%/day (Wilkinson 2021 cohort: half-volume in 86 days).
